Privacy Policy — M4CS

Last updated: 2 August 2026

M4CS is developed by Amrani Labs. This policy explains what happens to data when you use the app.

1.Data the app itself collects

None. Your projects, tasks, work sessions, targets, revenue figures and statistics are stored only in the app's private storage on your own device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. M4CS has no server and no account system, and we cannot see any of your data.

If you enable Android's system backup, Google may include this local file in your personal Google backup. That is controlled entirely by your device settings.

2.Advertising

M4CS displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • device information and ad interaction data

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller. See Google's policy for partner sites and apps.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form the first time it runs, and you can change your choice at any time from the app menu under Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your Android settings.

3.Permissions

  • Internet / network state — required to load ads
  • Notifications — to show your running-session timer and the optional evening planning reminder
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob

The app requests no access to contacts, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

4.Children

M4CS is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

5.Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes everything it stored on your device. Because nothing is held on a server, there is no other copy to request the deletion of.

Any future change to this policy will be published at this address with a new date.
